How It Works

Getting Started

1

Choose your company — Select DACP Holdings, DACP Constructions, or Veho Hospitality. This sets the logo on the screensaver.

2

Pick a template — 14 options ranging from dark textures (Obsidian, Onyx, Noir) to light tones (Ivory, Linen, Slate). Each has its own color palette and texture effects.

3

Enter your details — Fill in your name, job title, email, phone, and LinkedIn URL. The LinkedIn URL generates a scannable QR code on the wallpaper.

4

Customize sizes — Use the Name Size slider to adjust the name font (or leave Auto-fit on). The Contact Size slider controls email and phone text.

5

Choose QR style — Classic (standard squares), Rounded (soft corners), or Dots (circular modules).

6

Select your device — Pick your phone model from the Export Size dropdown for a pixel-perfect lock screen image.

7

Download — Hit Download PNG to save the wallpaper. Then set it as your lock screen in your phone’s settings.

Send to Phone

Click Send to Phone to generate a QR code containing a link with all your settings pre-filled. Scan it with your phone camera to open the generator on your mobile browser with everything ready — just tap Download.

Note: Send to Phone requires the app to be hosted on a web server (e.g. Netlify, S3). It won’t work when opened as a local file.

Setting as Lock Screen

iPhone: Open the downloaded image in Photos → tap Share → Use as Wallpaper → choose Lock Screen → Set.

Android: Open the image in Gallery → tap Menu (three dots) → Set as Wallpaper → Lock Screen.

Template Textures

Templates include combinations of these effects: fine grain, concrete noise, linen weave, brushed metal lines, edge vignette, center spotlight, and aurora color bleed. All effects export to the PNG.

Tip: For best QR scannability, use the Classic QR style. Rounded and Dots look great but may scan slower on older phones. Make sure your LinkedIn URL is correct before sharing!
